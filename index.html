💡 Before You Start Your Interview

6 Essential Preparation Pillars
📄

Know Your Resume

Be prepared to explain every skill, project, internship, and achievement mentioned in your resume.

💡 Tip: Revisit the specific metrics & tech stack for your lead project.
🧠

Revise DSA

Prepare common data structures, algorithms, complexity analysis, and problem-solving questions.

💡 Tip: Focus on Two Pointers, Hash Maps, and Binary Search Trees.
💻

Understand Your Projects

Be ready to explain your project architecture, technologies, challenges, and your contribution.

💡 Tip: Emphasize trade-offs you made when resolving bottlenecks.
🗣️

Communicate Clearly

Explain your thought process step by step instead of giving only the final answer.

💡 Tip: Use the STAR methodology (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
⏱️

Manage Your Time

Think carefully but avoid spending too much time on one question.

💡 Tip: Spend ~2 minutes structuring your answer before speaking.
🎤

Stay Confident

Speak clearly and remain calm when answering difficult questions.

💡 Tip: Maintain a natural cadence and steady conversational pace.
